# SurveyAgent Color Theme: "Trust & Precision"

This color palette is designed specifically for insurance adjusting platforms, loss adjusters, and field surveyors. It prioritizes trustworthiness, clear legibility under varied outdoor lighting conditions, and corporate authority. It deliberately avoids neon space-purples, electric violet gradients, and other trendy "AI studio" aesthetics in favor of a clean, stable, and professional appearance.

---

## 1. Core Color Swatches

### A. Primary Corporate Brand Colors
- **Corporate Navy (`#0A1D37`)**
  - **Usage:** Main headers, branding highlights, primary buttons, navigation banners.
  - **Rationale:** Deep navy blue is the global standard for insurance, finance, and risk assessment. It instills confidence and stability.
- **Steel Blue (`#2A4365`)**
  - **Usage:** Secondary accents, selected items, active button hover states.
  - **Rationale:** A balanced blue that provides contrast against dark background canvases without looking overly colorful or playful.

### B. Base Canvas & Card Backgrounds (Dark Mode Focus)
- **Deep Slate Charcoal (`#0F172A`)**
  - **Usage:** Main application canvas background.
  - **Rationale:** Reduces eye-strain during outdoor dictation and inspection, offering high-contrast readability against text.
- **Card Navy-Slate (`#1E293B`)**
  - **Usage:** Info blocks, case list cards, checklist group backgrounds.
  - **Rationale:** Slightly lighter than the base background, creating clear structural hierarchy for form items.
- **Border Muted (`#334155`)**
  - **Usage:** Clean hairline borders, field dividers, input box outlines.

### C. Insurance Status & Severity Accents
- **Compliance Teal (`#0F766E`)**
  - **Usage:** Approved reports, "GOOD" photo quality metrics, completed checklist sections, validated records.
  - **Rationale:** A deep teal-green that feels clinical, precise, and verified, rather than an aggressive bright green.
- **Warning Amber (`#B45309`)**
  - **Usage:** Review pending states, "FAIR" quality issues, required missing fields.
  - **Rationale:** A solid corporate gold/amber that stands out clearly as requiring attention.
- **Damage Crimson (`#991B1B`)**
  - **Usage:** "SEVERE" damage level indicators, "POOR" photo overrides, "URGENT" priority cases, error states.
  - **Rationale:** A deep crimson red representing critical alerts and severe property loss.

---

## 2. Text Hierarchy (Maximum Legibility)

- **Primary Text (`#F8FAF4`)**: Crisp off-white for maximum contrast against deep slate backgrounds.
- **Secondary Text (`#94A3B8`)**: Cool steel gray for field labels, subtitles, and captions.
- **Muted Details (`#64748B`)**: Muted gray for timestamps, case IDs, and inactive info.
